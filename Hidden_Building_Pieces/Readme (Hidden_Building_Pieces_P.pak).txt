Hidden_Building_Pieces_P.pak
----------------------------------------------------------------------
Mod Version: 4.6.1
Author: AgentKush
Compatible with Icarus: All Weeks
Mod Type: EXMOD

## Description:
The game's 20 unreleased building pieces - pieces it has but no item lets you build - as
craftable items: Wood, Thatch and Interior Wood frames (Frame, Half Frame, Pillar), 7 pillars
(Clay Brick, Glass, Reinforced Glass, Aluminium, Scoria Brick, Scoria, Stone Brick) and
Concrete and Aluminium corner stairs. Each is crafted exactly like the game's matching item for
its material (same cost, bench, blueprint unlock and DLC). No vanilla row is changed.

## Changelog:

Ver 4.6.1
New icon and banner, drawn from the mod's live data. No gameplay change.

Ver 4.6
Now only the game's unreleased building parts. Removed 28 re-skins of parts the game does
not have; added the other 14 unreleased parts (Wood, Thatch and Interior Wood frames, the
Scoria pillar, Concrete and Aluminium corner stairs). Every item crafts like the game's
matching item for its material. Removed the extra Interior Wood Floor recipe and the edits
that changed every vanilla building (hammer repair and the wind immunity that also removed
insulation). Pieces built from the removed items can disappear from saves.

Ver 4.5
Rebuilt for the November 2026 frame rework. 5 frames used blueprints the update deleted and
now use the game's current frame blueprints. The Iron frames, Sandworm frames and Metal Grate
quarter floor asked for items that do not exist (Iron_Ingot, Sandite) and could not be crafted;
they now cost what the game charges for its own Iron beams, Sandworm beams and metal grate floor.
Fixed stability rows, piece types, materials and icons the game renamed or removed, and removed
66 leftover rows from the stairs dropped in 4.4.

Ver 4.4
Removed 28 pieces now official in vanilla (Corner Stairs, Stairs, curved Wood - from the Week 142 and Week 234 updates). 34 mod-exclusive pieces remain (frames + non-curved diagonals + metal grate floor). Updated all mesh references and removed a stray Mesh_Generic_Ice override.

Ver 4.1
Added the Interior Wood Floor recipe at the Fabricator and Manufacturer - the "forgotten
floor" that only existed at the Carpentry Bench, with mismatched data table names.

Ver 4.0
MAJOR OVERHAUL: Rebuilt from scratch. Removed all items now live in vanilla (Kepler update): curved sets, diagonal sets, wall curved, roof curved angles, advanced beams, diagonal curved wall angles for ClayBrick/Concrete/Glass/TemperedGlass/Scoria/ScoriaBrick/StoneBrick/Stone/Ice/Limestone. Removed all 15 RoofPeak items (now in base game). Removed broken Ice_Floor_TrapDoor, Ice_Roof_Half_Pitch, Ice_Stairs. Split remaining items from sets into 62 individual pieces. Fixed all recipes with proper bench assignments.

Ver 3.0.1
Crash fix: Fixed 60 recipe outputs from D_ItemsStatic to D_ItemTemplate.

Ver 3.0.0
Removed 44 items now in base game (Kepler update).

Ver 2.13.7
Added 675 new building pieces from DLC update.
