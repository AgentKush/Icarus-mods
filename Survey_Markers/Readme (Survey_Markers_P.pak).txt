Survey_Markers
----------------------------------------------------------------------
Mod Version: 1.0
Author: AgentKush
Compatible with Icarus: All Weeks
Mod Type: EXMOD (data)

## Description:
Adds the Survey Marker, a cheap and light Portable Beacon for marking ore, camps and cave entrances. Craft it by hand or at the Crafting Bench with no unlock: 6 Stick, 10 Fiber and 4 Stone make 2. It weighs 0.5 kg (the beacon weighs 10 kg), stacks to 20 and shows no "Must Be Placed Outside" warning under cover; like the beacon, it needs fairly level ground. Interact with a placed marker to set its name (up to 30 characters), icon (39), colour (10), compass view distance (0m to NO LIMIT) and Visible to Owner Only, in the same window as the Portable Beacon. The map shows every marker at any distance (unless another player has set it to Visible to Owner Only), with the name, owner and distance when you hover its icon; the compass shows the icon within the range set on the marker. That range is one setting for all players, new markers start at 500m, and a marker switched to NO LIMIT stays hidden for teammates who were outside its old range until they rejoin. The owner is whoever last changed the marker, and anyone can change or pick up any marker. To the game a marker is a Portable Beacon, so it counts in 24 mission steps that ask for a deployed beacon (not in WATERFALL or GEO-SURVEY: Canyons, which check for the Portable Beacon item); in DISPATCH it finishes the beacon step without a crafted Portable Beacon. Removing the mod removes every Survey Marker: placed ones stop appearing, and carried or stored ones are deleted. On a dedicated server, install the mod on the server too. Adds rows to Items-D_ItemsStatic.json, Items-D_ItemTemplate.json, Traits-D_Itemable.json, Traits-D_Deployable.json and Crafting-D_ProcessorRecipes.json; no vanilla row is changed.

Ver 1.0
Initial release. Survey Marker: a Portable Beacon crafted by hand or at the Crafting Bench from 6 Stick, 10 Fiber and 4 Stone, 2 per craft, 0.5 kg, stacks to 20, no outside warning.

  1. Install JimK72's Icarus Mod Manager
  2. Download Survey_Markers.EXMODZ
