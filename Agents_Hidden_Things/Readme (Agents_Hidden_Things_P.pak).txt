Agents_Hidden_Things_P.pak
----------------------------------------------------------------------
Mod Version: 1.0

Author: AgentKush

Compatible with Icarus: All Weeks

Mod Type: EXMOD

## Description:
Adds 57 items the game has in its files but never hands out to one Workshop tab, Agent's
Hidden Things: Marrowraith, Thornet, Felixus, Vineshade and Malformation gear, the MX-7 and
Tri-Barrel launchers, CHAC guns, Radiation and EMP grenades, Camo and Synthetic backpacks,
genetic injectors, Shengong and Xigo workshop tools and more. Workshop gear is bought
directly; everything else comes as a kit you open after landing.


Ver 1.0
Initial release. 57 unreleased items (17 bought directly, 40 as kits), with the item rows
the game left out and fixes to their unreleased rows: real names for "[DNT]" placeholders,
missing icons, the Thornet Pistol's fire rate, the Marrowraith Bolt's spawn point, the Building
Upgrade Tool's swap button, the DNA Serum's resistance, the Camo and Synthetic Backpacks'
repair item, the Felixus Gauntlets' arms, and a Woolly Mammoth talent for the heavy saddle.
