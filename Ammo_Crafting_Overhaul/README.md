## Features

### Mega Arrow Batches (50x) — 20 recipes
Craft 50 arrows at once, for the materials 50 cost in the game, at the game's stations (the hand-crafted ones also at the Crafting Bench):
- Stone, Bone, Fire, Flare, Poison (Character/Crafting Bench)
- Flint, Steel, Obsidian, Caveworm (Anvil Bench+)
- Aluminium, Carbon, Cold Steel, Black Wolf, Drill, Sandworm (Anvil T3+)
- Composite, Titanium, Iron Wood, Lithium, Uranium (Anvil T4)
- Black Wolf, Caveworm, Drill and Sandworm batches need the same blueprint as the game's recipe (the game grants those per account). Every other batch in this mod needs no blueprint

### Mega Bolt Batches (50x) — 10 recipes
Every craftable crossbow bolt, 50 at once:
- Copper, Metal, Steel, Obsidian (Anvil Bench+)
- Cold Steel, Platinum (Anvil T3+)
- Titanium, Iron Wood, Lithium, Uranium (Anvil T4)

### Mega Bullet Batches (50x) — 7 recipes
- Ammo Casings, Crude Ammo
- Pistol, Rifle, Assault Rifle rounds
- Shotgun Buckshot and Slugs

### Mega Special Ammo (50x) — 22 recipes
Six special ammo types for pistol, rifle, assault rifle and shotgun:
- **Explosive** — Pistol/Rifle/AR/Shotgun
- **Incendiary** — Pistol/Rifle/AR
- **Armor Piercing** — Pistol/Rifle/AR
- **Obsidian** — Pistol/Rifle/AR/Shotgun
- **Cold Steel** — Pistol/Rifle/AR/Shotgun
- **Iron Wood** — Pistol/Rifle/AR/Shotgun

### Mega Sniper Ammo (50x) — 2 recipes
- **Explosive** and **Incendiary** 12.7mm rounds for the legendary Anti-materiel Rifle (Machining Bench/Fabricator)
- The game has no item row for the explosive round, so the mod adds it (the same row Agent's Hidden Things uses)

### Ammo Conversion — 8 recipes
Switch between ammo types at Machining Bench/Fabricator:
- Pistol ↔ Rifle ↔ AR (20 pistol = 10 rifle = 10 AR)
- Buckshot ↔ Slug (10 buckshot = 5 slugs)

## Compatibility

- ✅ Compatible with Faster Crafting (stacks — fast AND bulk)
- ✅ Compatible with Stack Size Overhaul

## Installation

Import `Ammo_Crafting_Overhaul.EXMODZ` via Icarus Mod Manager.

## Version History

- **v1.2.3** - Every craftable arrow and bolt now has a 50x batch: 9 more arrow types (Fire, Flare, Poison, Caveworm, Black Wolf, Drill, Sandworm, Lithium, Uranium) and all 10 bolts, for 69 recipes in all. Each batch costs what 50 cost in the game and is made at the game's benches; the hand-crafted Fire, Flare and Poison arrows can also be made at the Crafting Bench, like Stone and Bone. The Black Wolf, Caveworm, Drill and Sandworm batches need the same blueprint as the game's own recipe, which the game grants per account; every other batch needs no blueprint, as before. The counts in the docs are fixed too: the special-ammo section has 22 recipes, not 24.
- **v1.2.2** - The 50x explosive 12.7mm round recipe works: adds the explosive sniper round's item row, which the game left out, so the recipe no longer makes a broken item.
- **v1.2.1** - New icon and banner, drawn from the mod's live data. No gameplay change.
- **v1.2** - Added 50x Sniper special-ammo batches (Explosive, Incendiary).
- **v1.1** - Crash fix: Fixed 48 recipe outputs from D_ItemsStatic to D_ItemTemplate (prevents crash when opening crafting stations).

- **v1.0** - Initial release, 50 recipes
