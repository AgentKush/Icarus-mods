Ammo_Crafting_Overhaul_P.pak
----------------------------------------------------------------------
Mod Version: 1.2.3

Author: AgentKush

Compatible with Icarus: All Weeks

Mod Type: EXMOD

## Description:
69 new ammo recipes: 50x batches of every craftable arrow (20 types) and bolt (10 types), 7 basic bullet and casing batches and 24 special-round batches, plus 8 ammo conversion recipes.



Ver 1.2.3
Every craftable arrow and bolt now has a 50x batch: 9 more arrow types (Fire, Flare, Poison,
Caveworm, Black Wolf, Drill, Sandworm, Lithium, Uranium) and all 10 bolts, for 69 recipes in all.
Each batch costs what 50 cost in the game and is made at the game's benches; the hand-crafted
Fire, Flare and Poison arrows can also be made at the Crafting Bench, like Stone and Bone. The
Black Wolf, Caveworm, Drill and Sandworm batches need the same blueprint as the game's own
recipe, which the game grants per account; every other batch needs no blueprint, as before.
The counts in the docs are fixed too: the special-ammo section has 22 recipes, not 24, and this
file said 48 recipes.

Ver 1.2.2
The 50x explosive 12.7mm round recipe works. Its output, the explosive sniper round, had no item
row in the game, so the recipe could only make a broken item. The mod now adds that row (the same
one Agent's Hidden Things uses, modelled on the game's incendiary sniper round). The round is for
the legendary Anti-materiel Rifle: 500 damage plus a 400-damage blast.

Ver 1.2.1
New icon and banner, drawn from the mod's live data. No gameplay change.

Ver 1.2
Added 50x batch crafting for Sniper special ammo (Explosive, Incendiary), which were previously missing.

Ver 1.1
Crash fix: Fixed 48 recipe outputs from D_ItemsStatic to D_ItemTemplate. Prevents EXCEPTION_ACCESS_VIOLATION when opening crafting stations.
Ver 1.0
Initial release, 50 recipes.

## Files Modified:
